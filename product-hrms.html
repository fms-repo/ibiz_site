AIVizion HRMS

Enterprise Application Oman & GCC AIBizs Product

AIVizion HRMS centralizes people operations with payroll automation, leave management, recruitment pipelines, appraisals, and employee self-service — tailored for Omani organizations.

People Operations, Simplified

Manage hiring, onboarding, compensation, attendance, and offboarding in one HR platform. HR teams gain dashboards for workforce analytics while employees access self-service tools for leave, documents, and personal records.

Integrated with ERP and payroll compliance requirements, with AI-assisted workflows and multi-company support for growing organizations.
