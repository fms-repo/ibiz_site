OTA Approved ASP & PEPPOL E-Invoicing

OTA Approved ASP PEPPOL Approved Oman Based

AIBizs is an OTA-approved Accredited Service Provider (ASP) and a PEPPOL-approved e-invoicing provider. We help organizations in Oman and the wider region meet tax authority requirements, automate compliant invoicing, and exchange electronic invoices through PEPPOL standards.

OTA-Approved Accredited Service Provider

As an OTA-approved ASP, AIBizs enables organizations to issue and manage electronic invoices in line with Oman Tax Authority requirements. Our platform supports validation, submission, and audit-ready records so finance teams stay compliant without manual rework.

We integrate e-invoicing into your existing ERP and business workflows, reducing errors, accelerating billing cycles, and giving leadership clear visibility into tax and invoicing status.

PEPPOL-Approved E-Invoicing

Our PEPPOL approval means your organization can exchange standardized electronic invoices through the PEPPOL network—supporting secure, interoperable document exchange with trading partners locally and internationally.

From invoice generation and digital signatures to delivery and status tracking, we provide an end-to-end e-invoicing solution built for regulatory compliance and operational efficiency.
