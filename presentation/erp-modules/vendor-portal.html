Vendor Portal

Self-Service Vendor Management

Activities & Features

Purchase Order View Vendors can view and download purchase orders assigned to them
Invoice Submission Vendors can submit invoices electronically with document attachments
Payment Status Track payment status and view payment history for submitted invoices
Profile Management Vendors can update their profile information, contacts, and banking details
Statement View View account statements with outstanding balances and transaction history
Notifications Receive notifications for new POs, invoice status updates, and payments
Secure Access Role-based access control with secure authentication and data encryption
Communication Direct messaging and communication channel with procurement team
