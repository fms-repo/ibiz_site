IVMS

Enterprise Application Oman & GCC AIBizs Product

AIBizs IVMS delivers live GPS tracking, journey planning, driver management, fines configuration, and advanced reporting — with HSE system integration for safer fleet operations.

Fleet Visibility & Control

Track vehicles in real time on live maps, plan routes with area-based alerts, and manage driver profiles, licenses, and performance. Operations teams get dashboards for fleet utilization, incidents, and compliance.

IVMS integrates with QHSE for journey planning and safety workflows, making it ideal for logistics, oil & gas, construction, and government transport fleets across Oman.
