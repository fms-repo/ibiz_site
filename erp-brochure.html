Platform overview

Enterprise operations, unified

AIBizs ERP centralizes finance, supply chain, customer and supplier management, manufacturing, projects, and HR — in one connected platform for Oman and the GCC.

Finance gets ledger control and consolidations. Operations get inventory and workflow clarity. Commercial teams get CRM and SRM. Leadership gets real-time visibility with role-based access.

  • End-to-end enterprise processes in one system
  • AI-ready workflows across modules
  • Role-based access for finance, ops, and leadership
  • Multi-company and multi-branch support
