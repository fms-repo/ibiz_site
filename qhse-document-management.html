Controlled Document Library

Organize HSE policies, SOPs, permits, forms, and manuals in a structured library so teams find the right document fast — and always the approved revision.

Version Control & Approvals

Publish only approved revisions. Track draft → review → approve → release with clear ownership so obsolete documents are never used in the field.

  • Version history with change notes
  • Review and approval workflows
  • Obsolete document control
  • Read-and-acknowledge for critical procedures

Access, Distribution & Audit Trail

Control who can view, edit, or approve documents, distribute updates to sites, and keep a full audit trail for clients, ISO, and regulatory reviews.
